Project 01

Stakeholder Communication Translator (AI Chatbot)

An AI-powered chatbot I designed and built to help technical professionals translate complex AI/ML concepts into clear, business-relevant language for non-technical stakeholders.

View live demo →
Description

This chatbot takes a technical concept or project update as input, identifies who the audience is, then produces a jargon-free explanation paired with a specific line on why the concept matters to that audience's goals (revenue, efficiency, risk, etc.), and offers to adjust the explanation further.

It was designed and tested using a design thinking framework (Empathy, Problem, Ideation, Prototype, Test) in ChatGPT, then deployed as a live, publicly accessible agent on Chatbase.co.

Objective

To address a real, recurring problem in AI/ML adoption: technical teams often explain what is changing without explaining why it matters, which stalls stakeholder buy-in. This project let me apply change-leadership research directly to a working tool.

Process

I identified the problem through empathy-mapping who is affected (technical staff communicating with non-technical stakeholders), defined a clear problem statement, considered multiple bot ideas before selecting this one, planned the bot's behavior across ten design questions, wrote a structured system prompt using a Context/Task/Constraints framework, and tested it against two different audiences (a VP of Sales and a warehouse operations manager) to confirm it adapted its language and business framing correctly.
